01 The program 훈련

Two tracks.
One dojang.

Whether you want to build fitness, learn real self-defense, or fight for a podium spot, there's a path for you. High school students can start from zero in undertraining and work up to varsity. College athletes train and compete as one varsity team.

06 Our mission 정신

We don't just train kicks. We train character.

Through Taekwondo we nurture discipline, strength and respect. Our instructors guide students physically and mentally, building integrity, perseverance and self-control.

  • Foster a supportive community where students challenge and encourage each other.
  • Build confidence through effective self-defense skills and better fitness.
  • Promote mental resilience and a positive attitude toward growth.
  • Develop leadership and teamwork, inside and outside the dojang.
  • Offer competitive training grounded in sportsmanship and goal-setting.

Who can join

Your first class
is one message away.

High school students can join undertraining as complete beginners or try out for the high school varsity. College students join through the college varsity, selected on skill and performance.

The program is exclusive to HAU students and is not open to the general public.
