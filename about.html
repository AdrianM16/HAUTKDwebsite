01 About us

Built at HAU, for Angelites

The HAU Taekwondo Sports Program offers complete training for high school and college students at Holy Angel University. High school students can start as beginners in undertraining and work up to varsity, while college athletes compete as the college varsity. Across every squad we focus on physical fitness, mental discipline and a real sense of community.

Whether you want to get fit, learn self-defense, or compete at a higher level, we'll support you every step of the way.

04 Training levels

Find your squad

High school has two squads, undertraining and varsity. College has one: the varsity.

High school

High School Undertraining

The starting point for high school students. Builds the foundation of skill, fitness and mental resilience, and prepares students to move up to the high school varsity.

Focus
Technique, fitness and mental conditioning.
Progression
A clear pathway from undertraining to the High School Varsity.
Support
Personal feedback and guidance toward your goals.
High school

High School Varsity

The high school competition team. Represents HAU in interschool and regional tournaments, with regular sessions that sharpen technique and prepare athletes for the next level.

Selection
Skill improvement, dedication to practice and sportsmanship.
Competitions
Interschool and regional tournaments, plus local meets.
Recognition
Awards for participation and improvement that grow a love for the sport.
College

College Varsity

College Taekwondo at HAU is varsity only. The team represents Holy Angel University at major Taekwondo competitions. Athletes keep high standards and train hard to stay competitive.

Selection
Past competition results, technical skill and physical fitness.
Competitions
Regional, national and intercollegiate tournaments.
Goal
Winning titles and building HAU's reputation in collegiate Taekwondo.

05 Training philosophy 정신

A journey, not a workout

At HAU, Taekwondo is a path of personal growth and self-discovery. We build discipline, strength and community, guided by the five tenets every student learns.

예의Courtesy

Respect for coaches, teammates and opponents.

염치Integrity

Knowing right from wrong, and choosing right.

인내Perseverance

Show up again tomorrow, even after a hard loss.

극기Self-Control

Power is nothing without control.

백절불굴Indomitable Spirit

Knocked down a hundred times, rise a hundred and one.
